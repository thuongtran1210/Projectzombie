# Băng Hà laser

Băng Hà's normal attack uses `DefenseTowerAttackMode.Laser`. It fires one, two or three beams at levels one, two and three. It keeps its existing damage per beam, attack interval and range at each tier. Three beams focused on one enemy deal three times that tier's per-beam damage; this is an intentional increase over the old single projectile. The 30% slow for two seconds is applied once per damaged enemy per pulse.

Selection starts with the primary target, then finds distinct enemies using the existing target mask, spatial selector and line-of-sight checks. Remaining beams focus the primary when fewer enemies are available. One target receives one aggregated damage callback; target leases are captured before resolving any target. Paused, dead, disabled or out-of-range targets cannot be hit. A laser pulse does not acquire a projectile slot or wait for projectile travel.

The attack uses the existing session effect service. A timed 0.24-second beam request captures its start/end at firing, not a lasting target reference. The shader's energy bands, fractures, bright core and cyan halo use service progress rather than wall-clock shader time. Two camera-facing flares add a six-ray flash, expanding ring and soft glow at the emitter and impact. They keep depth testing, with a small camera-facing surface offset to avoid burying the impact flare inside an enemy. Hit shards use the existing ice impact profile. The effect is a pulse rather than a continuous damage channel.

Three stored beam handles belong to each tower. A new pulse, disable, death, cleanup or restart releases those handles. Pausing freezes the service lifetime. Missing/rejected cosmetic requests do not cancel accepted laser damage. No per-shot GameObjects, materials or meshes are created by the beam visual itself; the existing service may grow its VFX pool after the small prewarm, and still enforces its active-effect budget. Android frame rate and overdraw remain unmeasured.

**Build Ice Laser** (`build-ice-laser` through `Tools/Invoke-UnityBridge.ps1`) rebuilds only the ice laser assets, updates the existing Băng Hà attack/data assets and renders `scratch/IceLaserPreview.png`. The legacy ice projectile definition remains as the source of the shared hit profile and for old previews; it is not prewarmed or launched by this tower in the match. Nỏ Thần and Hỏa Long retain their projectile attacks.

The modular tower validation includes level 1/2/3 beam counts, damage distribution with one and three targets, one slow per target, pause rejection, shader validity and visual reset/reuse. The Play Mode smoke additionally checks actual active beam counts, no ice projectile acquisitions, tier upgrades, paused beam retention and restart cleanup. It captures `scratch/IceLaserLevel1.png`, `scratch/IceLaserLevel2.png` and `scratch/IceLaserLevel3.png` from the running map. The isolated preview shows three distinct targets at tier three; those Play captures also exercise beams focused on one target.
